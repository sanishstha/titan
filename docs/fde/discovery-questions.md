# Discovery call questions (30 minutes)

Mostly listen. Take notes verbatim where you can. Do not pitch for the first 15 minutes.

## Their workflow today
1. Walk me through the last time you looked into a company. What did you do first, second, third?
2. Where do you get your information? Which sources do you trust the most?
3. How long does it take? Which step is the slowest or most annoying?
4. How do you keep track of what you already know about a company?

## Pain and stakes
5. What have you missed in the past that you wish you had caught earlier?
6. What happens when you get it wrong?
7. What do you do today to double-check a claim?

## Trust and limits
8. What would the tool have to show you before you believed a claim?
9. What would you never want it to do on its own?
10. If it was wrong, how would you want to find out?

## Fit with the tool
11. (After a short live demo) What is confusing? What is missing? What would you ignore?
12. Which view would you open first? Which would you never open?
13. What would make you come back next week?

## Constraints
14. Who else would need to see or approve the output?
15. Any restrictions on tools or data you can use (employer rules, compliance)?

## Close
16. Can I check back in two weeks after I change things based on this?
