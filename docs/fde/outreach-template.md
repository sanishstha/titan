# Outreach templates

Send to about 10 people. Expect 2-3 replies. Personalize the first line.

## Where to look
- Investing communities (Reddit, Discord, local investment clubs)
- Friends or family who follow companies or work in finance
- An accountant, analyst or finance student you know
- Small business owners with a real, boring problem (for the optional small-business pilot)

## Short message (DM or email)

> Hi [name], I built a tool that tracks what changes in a company's filings and shows the evidence behind each change. Would you be up for trying it for 30 minutes on a company you already follow and telling me what's useless or confusing? It's free, and I'm mostly looking for honest feedback.

## Follow-up if no reply after a week

> No worries if the timing isn't right. If you're ever curious, I can send a 2-minute screen recording first so you can see what it does before deciding.

## Small-business pilot message

> Hi [name], I'm building practical AI tools and I'd like to help one small business with a real problem for free as a learning project. If you have something repetitive or annoying (reconciling orders, answering the same emails, cleaning a spreadsheet), could we talk for 20 minutes about it? No obligation.

## Rules
- Be clear it is a learning project, not a product with a support team.
- Never promise results. Never touch their accounts or data without a clear written OK on what you can access.
- Do not ask anyone to use real money based on the tool's output.
